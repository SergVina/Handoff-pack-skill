# Lens: websites

Load this for websites for a business or a person: landing pages, portfolios, company sites, booking pages, blogs, small online shops. Common among freelancers and small businesses; ask in plain language when the user is not technical.

## Extra interview questions

- **Purpose**: the one thing a visitor should do (call, book, buy, subscribe, read), and how the owner will know it works.
- **Pages and content**: list of pages; who writes the texts and provides the photos, logo and colors; whether the content is ready. Missing content is a pending item with the owner as responsible.
- **Look and feel**: websites they like and why, brand colors and fonts, tone of the texts.
- **Updates**: who will change texts, prices or photos later and how often. This decides whether an easy editing system is needed or plain files are enough.
- **Features**: contact form, bookings, payments, newsletter, blog, multiple languages, maps, chat. For each: where submissions or payments go and who gets notified.
- **Domain, hosting and email**: whether they own a domain, preferred hosting and budget, whether they need email addresses on their domain.
- **Legal**: privacy policy, cookie notice, legal notice and terms of sale where applicable; who provides those texts.
- **Search and sharing**: how people will find the site (search engines, social networks, ads), the main search phrases, and how it should look when shared.
- **Devices and accessibility**: mobile first, minimum accessibility level.
- **Measurement**: whether they want visit statistics and with which tool, considering privacy rules.

- In `02-ARCHITECTURE.md`: a **site map** (mermaid or ASCII) and the **hosting and domain setup**, with who owns each account.

## Task-splitting hints

First task: a live, minimal version of the home page with the real look and the main call to action, deployed where the owner can open it. Then the rest of the pages, then each feature (forms, bookings, payments) one at a time with its notifications, then legal pages, search optimization and statistics.
